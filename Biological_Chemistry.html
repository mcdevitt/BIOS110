4 Levels of Protein Structure (Click each level):
1° Primary Structure
Linear sequence of amino acids held by covalent peptide bonds.
2° Secondary Structure
Local α-helices and β-pleated sheets held by backbone H-bonds.
3° Tertiary Structure
Overall 3D shape formed by R-group interactions (hydrophobic, H-bonds, ionic, disulfide).
4° Quaternary Structure
Two or more folded polypeptide subunits assembled together (e.g., hemoglobin).
Primary (1°) Structure: Determined directly by the DNA gene sequence. Even changing a single amino acid R-group can alter how the protein folds at higher levels!
