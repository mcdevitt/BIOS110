The 5 Core DNA Extraction Steps (Today's Lab)

Method Limits + Technique

Adjust what happens at each of the 5 core processes from your BIOS 110 DNA Extraction Lab Manual. Notice how some variations are normal reagent/method quirks while others are technique details.

Step 1: Cell Lysis

Lysozyme + DNA Shield (Enzymes lab) + bead beating + Lysis Buffers A & B

Step 2: DNA Binding

High-salt conditions bind DNA onto white silica membrane in spin column

Step 3: Washing

Wash 1 & Wash 2 + 2-min empty dry spin to remove proteins, salts & ethanol

Step 4: Elution

20 µL low-salt Elution Buffer on membrane center + 2-min soak into yellow tube

Step 5: Pure gDNA Extract

Final DNA dissolved in yellow collection tube, labeled & stored for future labs
