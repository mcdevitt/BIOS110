Exam Trap Radar

3 Classic BIOS 110 Strand & Reading Frame Traps

1

5'→3' DNA Template Trap

RNA Polymerase reads the template strand 3' → 5' to synthesize mRNA 5' → 3'. If an exam gives you a 5' → 3' template strand (like Study Guide Q3), you must reverse it to 3' → 5' first! Never confuse the template strand with the non-template (coding) strand.

2

5' UTR & AUG Start Trap

Do not start translating at the very first 3 bases of mRNA! The ribosome binds the 5' end and scans 5' → 3' until it finds the first 5' AUG 3' start codon. Bases before AUG form the 5' UTR, and AUG itself IS translated into Met.

3

Stop Codon ≠ Amino Acid

UAA, UAG, and UGA do not code for an amino acid named "Stop." They recruit a protein Release Factor that terminates translation. Never count the stop codon in your polypeptide amino acid length!

Anticodon Trap Buster

tRNA Anticodon ↔ mRNA Codon Polarity Converter

Never look up a tRNA anticodon directly in the mRNA codon table! Always align antiparallel (3'→5' tRNA with 5'→3' mRNA) and read the mRNA codon 5' → 3'.

Wobble Rule Note: The 3rd base of the mRNA codon (3' end) pairs with the 1st base of the tRNA anticodon (5' end), allowing flexible "wobble" pairing so ~45 tRNAs can recognize all 61 sense codons.

The 3 RNA Workers in Translation

mRNA (The Blueprint): Carries codons read 5' → 3' from AUG to stop codon.
tRNA (The Adapter): Specific aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases charge each tRNA with its matching amino acid using ATP.
rRNA (The Factory & Ribozyme): Structural core of ribosome subunits AND catalyzes peptide bond formation (peptidyl transferase)!

Prokaryotic vs. Eukaryotic Gene Expression Lab

Compare coupled transcription-translation in prokaryotes with nuclear compartmentalization and RNA processing in eukaryotes. Toggle perturbations to test functional consequences!

Prokaryotes (Bacteria & Archaea)

Coupled in Cytoplasm
  • No nuclear envelope: Ribosomes attach to the 5' end of mRNA while RNA Polymerase is still transcribing the 3' end!
  • No introns, no 5' cap, no poly-A tail: Primary transcript is immediately mature mRNA.
  • Often organized into operons (polycistronic mRNA encoding multiple proteins).

Eukaryotes (Nucleus + Cytoplasm)

Spatially & Temporally Separated
  • Nuclear Envelope Barrier: Transcription & RNA processing occur inside the nucleus; translation occurs in the cytoplasm.
  • 5' Cap + 3' Poly-A Tail: Protect mRNA from exonucleases, aid nuclear pore export, and help small ribosomal subunit bind.
  • Spliceosome (snRNPs + proteins): Excises noncoding introns and joins expressed exons.
