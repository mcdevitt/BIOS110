Structure → Speed & ATP Efficiency

Myelinated vs. Unmyelinated Axon Conduction

Speed Advantage (up to 120 m/s)

Myelin prevents ion leakage; voltage-gated Na⁺ channels cluster exclusively at Nodes of Ranvier, so current leaps rapidly between nodes.

Metabolic ATP Savings

Because Na⁺ only enters at tiny nodes rather than the entire axon membrane, Na⁺/K⁺ pumps spend far less ATP restoring resting gradients.

STEP 1 OF 5 • ELECTRICAL Signal Conversion

Action Potential Reaches Synaptic Terminal

Depolarization travels down the axon to the presynaptic terminal bouton, triggering voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels in the terminal membrane to open.

Test Synaptic Perturbations (What if...) Causal Reasoning
Normal Transmission: Electrical AP → Ca²⁺ influx → neurotransmitter exocytosis → receptor binding → postsynaptic Na⁺ influx → new electrical impulse.
Why Switch from Electrical to Chemical at a Synapse?

Chemical synapses allow signal integration (summing excitatory + inhibitory inputs), gain/amplification, one-way directionality, and modulation (learning, drugs, and autonomic control)—whereas direct electrical wires would blindly fire every connected cell.
